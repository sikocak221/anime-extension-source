# Copyright and takedown

This repository contains source code only. It does not host, store, index or distribute any video, subtitle, image or other work.

If you are a rights holder, or act for one, and believe something in this repository infringes your rights, tell us and we will look at it promptly.

## How to report

Open a private report: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on [sikocak221/anime-extension-source](https://github.com/sikocak221/anime-extension-source/security/advisories/new). Please do not open a public issue.

Include:

- who you are and, if you act for someone, who that is;
- the work or mark concerned and where you hold the rights;
- where it is in this repository (a file, an extension name, or a link);
- how to reach you;
- a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized, and that the information is accurate.

## What happens

A valid request is answered within a few days. If it is justified, the extension or file concerned is removed from the repository and from the published index. A person who believes a request was made in error may reply to it with their reasons.

## Not a copyright matter

Problems with a third-party site's own content are for that site. This repository has no control over it.
